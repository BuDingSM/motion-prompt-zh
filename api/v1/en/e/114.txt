// Low-key / ローキー
// Pack: photo (Photo looks, lighting & styles) · element 18 · Category: Tones & finishing looks
// What happens: A still life on a grey background changes to a black background lit by a single light; only the lit sides stand out and most of the frame is dark. The histogram at bottom right shifts to the dark (left) side.
// Motion spec:
//   - Background: rgb(176,172,166) → rgb(12,12,13) (top); table → rgb(22,20,18)
//   - Light: from the top left; shadow side brightness 42% → 5%; cast shadows 38% → 85%
//   - Spot: a radial gradient centred at (270, 100) darkens the outside (55% black at half the radius, 92% at 240 px)
//   - Histogram: peaks at 0.3, 0.55, 0.78 → 0.03, 0.1, 0.55
//   - Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
// When to use it: For luxurious, tense or mysterious images.
// Tags: low-key, low key, dark, black background, moody, dramatic, chiaroscuro
