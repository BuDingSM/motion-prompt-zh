Motion Prompt #099

What it is: The subject shows as a black shape against a bright sky
What the sample shows: As the exposure is set for the sunset sky, the washed-out sky regains its colour while the person and the palm tree lose all colour and become pure black shapes.
Spec:
- Exposure: +1.0 EV → −2.0 EV (top-right readout)
- Sky: vertical gradient #3E2C78 → #C2507A → #F79A55 → #FFC46E; sun at x 372, y 126
- At +1 EV a 55% white rgb(255,246,236) layer washes the sky out
- At −2 EV the person (red top, 104 px tall), the palm and the beach all fall to rgb(12,8,16)
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: When the shape and pose should carry the picture, against a sunset or a bright window.
