Please use "Split lighting" (スプリットライティング), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #100

What it is: Light from the side splits the face into lit and dark halves
What the sample shows: A single light swings from the front to the side; the face divides along the nose, one half lit and the other in shadow.
Spec:
- Light: from 0° to 90° (right side), height stays at 5°; angles shown at top right
- Bust: a plaster-like head with no eyes or hair, only the relief of nose, brows, eye sockets, cheeks and chin; centre x 270, y 86
- Shading: diffuse light raised to the power 1.5 for a hard edge; cast shadows traced from each point toward the light; shadow side at 5%
- Look: light and dark divide along a vertical line through the nose; no light reaches the far cheek
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1))); a top-down lighting diagram sits at bottom right
When to use it: For dramatic, tense or moody portraits.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/100.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/photo.js
