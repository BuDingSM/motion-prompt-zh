Motion Prompt #102

What it is: High frontal light that casts a butterfly shadow under the nose
What the sample shows: A single light stays in front and rises; a symmetrical butterfly-shaped shadow forms right under the nose, with shadows under the cheekbones and chin.
Spec:
- Light: stays at 0° (straight in front) and rises from 5° to 45°; angles shown at top right
- Bust: a plaster-like head with no eyes or hair, only the relief of nose, brows, eye sockets, cheeks and chin; centre x 270, y 86
- Shading: diffuse light raised to the power 1.5 for a hard edge; cast shadows traced from each point toward the light; shadow side at 5%
- Look: a symmetrical shadow right below the nose, plus shadows under the brows, cheekbones and chin; both sides of the face are equally bright
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1))); a top-down lighting diagram sits at bottom right
When to use it: For beauty and fashion portraits that flatter the face.
