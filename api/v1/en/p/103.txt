Motion Prompt #103

What it is: The low, warm light just after sunrise or before sunset
What the sample shows: Time moves from noon (high sun, white light, short shadows) to just before sunset (sun near the horizon, warm light, long shadows to the right, the left edges of the person and tree glowing).
Spec:
- Clock: 12:00 → 17:40 (top-right readout); sun elevation 63° → 4°, on screen (150, −40) → (64, 104)
- Shadows: length = height × 1/tan(sun elevation) (at most 3×), running right and slightly down from the feet (drop = 0.16 × length), blurred 1.5 px
- Colour: sky near the horizon rgb(200,226,244) → rgb(255,196,118); rgb(255,186,110) soft-light at 75% over everything
- Edge light: about 2 px of orange #FFB060 added on the left edges of the person and tree (60%)
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For warm, nostalgic, gentle-feeling images.
