Please use "Blue hour" (ブルーアワー), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #104

What it is: The time after sunset or before sunrise when the sky turns deep blue
What the sample shows: From just after sunset (orange horizon, city lights off) the sky turns deep blue while building windows and waterfront lamps come on one by one and reflect in the water.
Spec:
- Clock: 18:00 → 18:35 (top-right readout)
- Sky: top rgb(86,118,176) → rgb(16,34,104), horizon rgb(255,178,112) → rgb(126,128,196)
- Lights: each window switches on at its own moment and brightens over 0.12 of the effect amount (warm rgb(255,206,120), some white rgb(210,230,255)); 8 waterfront lamps
- Water: the city mirrored at 45%, blurred 1.5 px, each 2 px strip shifted up to 3 px sideways for ripples
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For city and night scenes that need both a blue sky and glowing lights.
