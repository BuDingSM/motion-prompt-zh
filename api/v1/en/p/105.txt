Motion Prompt #105

What it is: Out-of-focus points of light melt into soft discs
What the sample shows: As the aperture opens, the in-focus person stays sharp while the string lights and windows behind turn into large, soft discs of light.
Spec:
- Aperture: f/16 → f/1.4 (top-right readout)
- Background: blur 0.4 px → 9 px (edges are extended outward before blurring)
- Point lights: 62; disc radius = (1.6 + blur × 1.9) × size; bigger discs are fainter; additive blending; a thin brighter rim
- Subject: the person at front left (x 175, head radius 31 px) stays sharp throughout
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: To lift a person or product off the background and add a soft, festive night mood.
