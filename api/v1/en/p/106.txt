Motion Prompt #106

What it is: Blur top and bottom so a real scene looks like a miniature
What the sample shows: A town seen from above; leaving only a horizontal band in the middle sharp, blurring above and below strongly and boosting the colour makes the real town look like a scale model. Small cars keep driving.
Spec:
- Town: seen from 95 m up, looking down 35.5° (focal length 380 px); buildings, trees and cars are simple boxes and circles
- Sharp band: 44–58% of the frame height, fading out at 30% and 72%
- Medium blur 2.4 px: 30–72% (fading out at 8% and 94%); 6.5 px beyond that
- Colour: saturation ×1.45, contrast ×1.1; cars drive at 12 m/s and move exactly one car spacing in 5 s
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: To make a city or event look like a toy model world.
