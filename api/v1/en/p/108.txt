Please use "Panning shot" (流し撮り), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #108

What it is: Follow a moving subject so only the background streaks
What the sample shows: The camera follows a moving car while the shutter gets slower; the car stays sharp and only the trees, buildings and road markings behind it streak sideways.
Spec:
- Shutter: 1/1000 s → 1/15 s (top-right readout)
- Background: scrolls left at 480 px/s (a 600 px repeating strip, 4 repeats in 5 s)
- Streak: 16 copies averaged across background speed × shutter time (0.5 px → 32 px)
- Car: holds still at the centre (x 270, y 172); the wheel spokes blur away at slow shutter speeds
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: To show the speed of a car, bike or runner in a still photo.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/108.txt
