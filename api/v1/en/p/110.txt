Please use "Fisheye lens" (魚眼レンズ), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #110

What it is: Ultra-wide lens that bulges the centre and bends straight lines
What the sample shows: From a normal lens (straight lines stay straight) to a fisheye: the centre bulges and building edges and road lines bend more the closer they are to the edges.
Spec:
- Distortion: the distance r from the centre maps to R·atan(c·r/R)/atan(c) (R = half the diagonal, 289 px; c 0.001 → 2.6)
- The centre is magnified about 2.2×, the corners stay put; lines are cut into pieces of 6 px or less before bending
- Readout: diagonal angle of view 84° → 180° (top right)
- Scene: a row of buildings with window grids, a pavement, road markings and 4 lamp posts
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For bold, playful or peephole-like views (skateboarding, music videos).
