Motion Prompt #113

What it is: A bright, white-toned image with very light shadows
What the sample shows: A still life on a grey background changes to a white background with bright light and very faint shadows; the histogram at bottom right shifts to the bright (right) side.
Spec:
- Background: rgb(176,172,166) → rgb(252,251,249) (top); table rgb(128,120,110) → rgb(236,233,228)
- Light: intensity 1 → 1.12; shadow side brightness 42% → 86%
- Cast shadows: 38% → 10% black (6 px blur)
- Histogram: peaks at 0.3, 0.55, 0.78 → 0.78, 0.9, 0.96 (0 = black, 1 = white)
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For clean, light, gentle product shots and portraits.
