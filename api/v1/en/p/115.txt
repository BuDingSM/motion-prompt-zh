Motion Prompt #115

What it is: Remove colour and show brightness only
What the sample shows: The colourful beach photo loses its colour and becomes a black-and-white image in which only differences in brightness show the shapes.
Spec:
- Brightness: 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B (Rec. 709)
- Contrast: ×1.18 around mid grey
- Cross-fades from the source photo (blue sky, sea, beach, red-and-white parasol, a person in orange) to the processed one; a slider at bottom right shows the amount
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For a timeless feel, or when shape, light and expression matter more than colour.
