Please use "Film grain" (フィルムグレイン), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #118

What it is: Fine, film-like grain over the image
What the sample shows: Fine grain covers the whole beach photo and the blacks lift slightly toward warm; the grain pattern keeps changing.
Spec:
- Grain: per-pixel grey noise (sum of three uniform randoms, ±96) blurred 0.45 px, overlay at 70%
- Motion: 4 fixed-seed patterns cycled 8 times a second (40 changes in 5 s)
- Colour: R = 22 + 0.88 R, G = 16 + 0.86 G, B = 10 + 0.8 B (lifted, warm blacks)
- A slider at bottom right shows the amount
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: To remove the clean digital look and add an analogue film texture.
