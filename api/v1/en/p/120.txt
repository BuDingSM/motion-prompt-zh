Please use "Double exposure" (二重露光（多重露光）), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #120

What it is: Two photos layered into one frame
What the sample shows: A forest-and-mountain landscape is screen-blended over a profile photo on white; the white stays white, the landscape shows only inside the dark profile, and the top of the head dissolves into the white sky.
Spec:
- First image: a right-facing head-and-shoulders profile silhouette (#22252C → #3A3E48) on #F3F1ED
- Second image: white sky at the top, mountains #9FB2B4, three rows of conifers (#4F6E6A, #2E4A46, #1A2C2A), 3 birds
- Blending: the second image is screen-blended from 0% to 100% (white stays white, the landscape appears in the dark areas)
- A slider at bottom right shows the amount
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.6 s to 1.7 s, holds until 3.9 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: For posters and covers that tie a person to a place, a memory or an inner world.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/120.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/photo.js
