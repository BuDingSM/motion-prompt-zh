請使用「定格（Freeze frame）」，它是「攝影機運動與轉場」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #040

它是什麼：讓動作停住一拍
範例展示：The picture stops at the moment a jumping person reaches the top, then resumes from the same frame 1.4 s later.
規格：
- Camera: completely fixed (height 1.3 m); what stops is the footage time
- Freeze: stops at 1.6 s and resumes at 3.0 s (1.4 s); footage time runs normally for 0–1.6 s, stays at 1.6 s while frozen, then continues as u-1.4
- Subject: the jump of a person 7 m away is a parabola over footage time 0.6–2.6 s, peak 1.1 m; the freeze lands exactly on the peak (50% through)
- Nine falling leaves also stop during the freeze; they fall 1.5× as fast as in 13 Slow motion
- Freeze markers: a 5 px blue border and a pause icon at bottom centre (two blue bars on a 68×40 px white rounded box), fading in and out over 0.12 s
- Top-right readout shows "FREEZE" only while frozen; no cross-fade reset
適用情境：To emphasise a decisive moment, or to pause so a name or caption can be placed over the frame.
