請使用「Toast（Toast notification）」，它是「UI 組件名稱」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #056

它是什麼：角落跳出通知並自動消失
範例展示：Clicking "Save" slides a dark notice into the bottom-right corner; when its time bar runs out it slides away.
規格：
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. The cursor clicks the blue "Save" button (110×36, bottom left) at 1.0 s and leaves to the bottom left.
- Toast: #26282c, 214×52, radius 10, 18 px from the right and 16 px from the bottom; slides in 240 px from the right over 1.05–1.4 s with easeOutCubic.
- Inside: a white check in a blue circle and white 15 px text "Saved".
- A time-left line along the bottom (3 px tall) shrinks linearly from 194 px to 0 over 1.4–3.2 s.
- Exit: slides 240 px out to the right over 3.2–3.55 s with easeInOutCubic.
適用情境：Use it to confirm a result such as a save or send without interrupting the user.
