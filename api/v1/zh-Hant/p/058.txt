Motion Prompt #058

它是什麼：橫向切換，依序展示內容
範例展示：Each click on the right arrow slides a row of three different images one step to the left, and the dot below moves.
規格：
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. Slides 330×138, radius 8, centred with an 18 px gap; the neighbours peek in at the edges.
- White round buttons (radius 18, shadow blur 10) with chevrons at left and right; three dots below (current one blue 16×8, others 8×8).
- The cursor clicks the right arrow (514, 88) at 1.0, 2.1 and 3.2 s.
- Each click moves the row 348 px left (one slide plus gap) over 0.4 s with easeInOutCubic.
- The three pictures (landscape, three circles, bar chart) repeat, so after three moves the first is back.
適用情境：Use it to show several images or featured items in limited space, one at a time.
