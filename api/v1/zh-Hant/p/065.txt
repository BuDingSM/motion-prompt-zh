請使用「漢堡選單（Hamburger menu）」，它是「UI 組件名稱」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #065

它是什麼：點擊三條線圖示打開選單
範例展示：Clicking the three-line icon at the header's right turns it into ×, and a menu drops down from under the header; clicking again closes it.
規格：
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A white 44-tall header; three lines (24 long, 3 thick, 8 px apart) at (500, 22) on the right.
- The cursor clicks at 1.0 s and 3.3 s. Open amount a: 0→1 over 1.0–1.3 s, 1→0 over 3.3–3.6 s, easeInOutCubic.
- Lines to ×: the top and bottom lines move 8 px to the centre and rotate ±45°; the middle line fades with opacity 1 − a. Blue while open.
- Menu: white, 140 tall, top at y = 44 − 140 + 140·a so it drops from under the header (clipped below the header), shadow blur 16, four items, the first blue.
適用情境：Use it on narrow screens to tuck the main navigation behind one button.
