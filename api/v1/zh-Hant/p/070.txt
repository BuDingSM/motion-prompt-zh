請使用「Spinner」，它是「UI 組件名稱」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #070

它是什麼：旋轉的圓圈表示請稍候
範例展示：Clicking "Send" washes the card white and a blue arc spins in the middle; when finished it changes to a check and fades.
規格：
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. The cursor clicks "Send" (120×40, bottom centre) at 1.0 s.
- Over 1.05–1.2 s (linear) a white scrim (opacity 0.78) and the spinner fade in over the whole card.
- Spinner at (271, 92): a pale ring, radius 26, 6 thick, with a blue 243° (1.35π) arc on top (round caps), turning 1.3 times per second (468°/s).
- Over 2.95–3.05 s the spinner fades; over 2.95–3.2 s a blue circle (radius 28) with a white check scales 0.7 → 1 (easeOutCubic).
- Everything fades out linearly over 3.75–4.0 s.
適用情境：Use it for short waits of unknown length, such as sending or saving.
