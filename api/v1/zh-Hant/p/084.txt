請使用「磁吸按鈕（Magnetic button）」，它是「落地頁與網站效果」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #084

它是什麼：遊標靠近時，按鈕會被吸過去
範例展示：As the cursor comes near, the button is drawn toward it and follows; when the cursor leaves, the button wobbles back to its place.
規格：
- A 168 × 52 button (orange, radius 26) in the centre. The pull range is a 110 px radius from the button's centre.
- Inside the range: target offset = (cursor − button centre) × 0.35; every frame (60 fps) the button covers 18% of the way to the target.
- On leaving the range it returns from its current offset to 0 over 1.1 s with elastic.out(1, 0.3): 2^(−10x)·sin((x − 0.075)·2π/0.3) + 1.
- The cursor enters from the left, circles the button and leaves to the right.
適用情境：Use it on the one button you most want clicked, such as sign-up or contact.
