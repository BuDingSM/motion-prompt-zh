請使用「Before/After 對比滑桿（Before/after slider）」，它是「落地頁與網站效果」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #087

它是什麼：拖曳分隔線，比較前後的差異
範例展示：Dragging the line in the middle of the photo left and right changes how much of the grey "before" (left) and the coloured "after" (right) is shown.
規格：
- Photo frame 359 × 140 (radius 10). Right: the edited photo in colour. Left: the same photo as the "before" (saturation removed, navy at 0.18 opacity on top).
- The before image is clipped to the left of the divider. On the divider: a white line (3 wide) and a white circle (radius 17) with left/right arrows.
- At 0.7 s the circle is grabbed; the divider goes 50%→20% (0.75–1.55 s) →82% (1.55–2.65 s) →50% (2.65–3.55 s), all cubic ease-in-out.
- Released at 3.62 s. Labels BEFORE (top left) and AFTER (top right).
適用情境：Use it on pages that sell a change: renovation, beauty, photo retouching.
