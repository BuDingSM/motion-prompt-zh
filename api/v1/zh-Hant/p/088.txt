請使用「Exit-intent 彈出視窗（Exit-intent popup）」，它是「落地頁與網站效果」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #088

它是什麼：訪客快離開頁面時跳出的慰留視窗
範例展示：When the cursor heads out through the top of the page (toward the tabs and close button), the page dims and a "Wait!" popup opens. The × closes it.
規格：
- The cursor rises from the page toward the browser's address bar at 0.7–1.25 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- It fires when the cursor crosses the top edge of the viewport (y = 0, at about 1.07 s), the same test as mouseleave on document with clientY ≤ 0.
- Opens 0.05–0.45 s after firing (cubic ease-out): backdrop navy 0→0.5 opacity; popup (260 × 132, radius 12) opacity 0→1 and scale 0.9→1.
- Inside: a heading, a discount line (orange), a coupon button and an × at the top right.
- Click on the × at 2.9 s; it disappears 0.03–0.25 s later (linear).
適用情境：Use it to make one last offer to visitors who are about to leave without converting.
