Motion Prompt #106

它是什麼：把上下兩側模糊，讓真實的街景看起來像模型
範例展示：The top and bottom bands of the street blur while only the middle band with the road, the car and the small people stays in focus, so the street looks like a model.
規格：
- The street corner (buildings, trees and lamps behind a road at y 132–162) without the large person; four small people 23–26 px tall stand on the pavement (feet at y 172); the car drives from x −60 to 600 every 5 s
- Focus band: y 66–176 stays sharp
- Blur: y 0–42 and y 190–208 go 0 → 7 px; y 42–66 and y 176–190 go 0 → 3 px
- Dashed lines on the band edges (6 px dash, 5 px gap, #8E8E93 at 75%)
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
適用情境：To make a city or event look like a toy model world.
