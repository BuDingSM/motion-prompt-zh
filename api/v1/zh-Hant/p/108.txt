請使用「追焦（Panning shot）」，它是「攝影風格、光線與鏡頭效果」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #108

它是什麼：跟著移動的被攝者拍，只讓背景拉出流動線條
範例展示：The camera follows the moving car: the car stays sharp in the middle of the frame while only the buildings, trees and lamps behind it streak sideways.
規格：
- Street corner: buildings, trees and lamps behind a road at y 132–162 (no large person in front)
- Car: without the effect it drives from x −80 to 620 in 5 s; with it, it stays at x 270 ±10 px
- Streak: the background is drawn 12 times spread over 46 px sideways and averaged (spread 0 → 46 px)
- Wheels: a spoke line turns 12 times per 5 s
- Top-right shutter speed 1/1000 → 1/15
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
適用情境：To show the speed of a car, bike or runner in a still photo.
