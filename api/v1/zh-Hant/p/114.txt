Motion Prompt #114

它是什麼：以暗色調爲主，只有受光處浮現出來
範例展示：Most of the frame sinks into darkness and the sun goes out; only the right edge of the bust, lit from the side, stays bright. The histogram hump moves to the left end.
規格：
- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right. 
- Colour: sky #FAF9F7 → #2B2D33, hills → #313339 and #36383E, ground → #24252A, figure → #25272E; the sun and its rings take the sky colour and disappear
- The lit right edge of the bust stays, #FFC75A → #FFB547
- Histogram: peak centre 0.56 → 0.14, width 0.2 → 0.08
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
適用情境：For luxurious, tense or mysterious images.
