請使用「底片顆粒（Film grain）」，它是「攝影風格、光線與鏡頭效果」中的一個名稱。
Motion Prompt #118

它是什麼：加上像底片相機拍出來的細緻顆粒
範例展示：Fine grain appears over the whole of the same landscape and the colours lean slightly towards a warm cream.
規格：
- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right. 
- Grain: 2,600 squares (fixed seed), 0.8–1.7 px; 55% dark #3A3530, 45% white
- Grain opacity: 16–38% × the effect; the grain does not move (no flicker)
- Colour: every colour moves 16% towards #F2DDBE
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
適用情境：To remove the clean digital look and add an analogue film texture.
