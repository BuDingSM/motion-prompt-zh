Motion Prompt #144

它是什麼：Divides the frame to show scenes side by side
範例展示：One full-frame scene splits into two panels, then three, each with its own scene moving.
規格：
- A full-frame sea scene splits into two panels at 0.45–0.95 s (divider 539→269.5), then into three at 1.4–1.9 s (dividers at 1/3 and 2/3), and merges back into one at 3.75–4.4 s, all cubic ease-in-out.
- The three scenes (sea, city, field) are each centred in and clipped to their own panel and keep moving inside it (waves and a sailboat, city windows switching 8 times per 4.7 s, a windmill turning twice per 4.7 s).
- White dividers (width 6) between the panels.
適用情境：Use it to compare, to show things happening at once, or to line up several people or places, such as before and after or a set of options.
