Motion Prompt #028

它是什么：摄影机不动，只用镜头放大
示例展示：The camera does not move; only the focal length gets longer, so the whole picture enlarges by the same ratio.
规格：
- Camera: position and direction fixed (height 1.5 m); only the focal length changes, 330 px → 1200 px (shown as 24mm → 87mm, about 3.6×)
- Exponential interpolation: f = 330 × (8/2.2)^p, where p is a cubic ease-in-out from 0.5 s to 3.4 s (2.9 s)
- Subject: a person stands still 8 m ahead; the final size matches 03 Dolly in (about 3.6×)
- No parallax: the foreground post and rock, the person, buildings and mountains all scale by the same ratio (overlaps do not change)
- Return: hold 3.4–4.2 s, then cross-fade back to the start over the last 0.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
- Top-right readout shows the focal length in mm (24mm → 87mm)
适用场景：To direct attention to one point without moving the camera; it looks flatter and more observational than a dolly in.
