Motion Prompt #045

它是什么：一条移动的边界把画面换掉
示例展示：A vertical edge moves from left to right and the next picture replaces the current one behind it.
规格：
- Join: 1.1 s, from 1.7 s to 2.8 s; the edge travels from just outside the left side (x = -4 px) to just outside the right side (x = 543 px)
- Easing: cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1))
- The incoming picture shows left of the edge and the outgoing one right of it; neither picture moves (a reveal by clipping, not a push)
- Edge: a 4 px blue vertical line, visible only while it is moving
- Camera: fixed in both pictures; the subjects do not move either
- Alternates A→B and B→A every 4.7 s (9.4 s for the round trip); top-right readout shows "A → B" / "B → A"
适用场景：To mark a clear change of scene or topic, or to switch between the two sides of a comparison.
