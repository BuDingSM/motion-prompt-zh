Motion Prompt #060

它是什么：用并排按钮切换检视
示例展示：Clicking Day, Week or Month slides a blue highlight across and the bar chart below changes height.
规格：
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A grey 300×40 base (radius 10) at (121, 12), split into three 100-wide segments: Day, Week, Month (in Japanese).
- Selection: blue 92×32, radius 8, shadow blur 6; the selected label is white.
- The cursor clicks Week at 1.0 s, Month at 2.1 s and Day at 3.3 s; the selection slides over 0.25 s each with easeInOutCubic.
- Chart below: seven bars 34 wide, 58 px apart (the sixth is blue). Heights move between three data sets (about 30–85, 40–100, 60–115) together with the selection, eased the same way.
适用场景：Use it to switch between two to four views of the same content, such as day, week and month.
