Motion Prompt #099

它是什么：以明亮的天空为背景，被摄者只呈现黑色的形状
示例展示：As the exposure is set for the bright evening sky, the person, buildings, lamp and tree turn into flat black shapes with no detail and only the sky stays bright.
规格：
- Scene 1: a head-and-shoulders figure in front of a horizon at y 150 (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px, no face or hair). 
- Sky: #FAF9F7 → #FFEDC4 at the top and a #FFCF73 band near the horizon (y 92–150)
- Everything but the sky becomes #2A2D36, the far hills #4A4546; the figure #22252D
- Sun: radius 20 px at x 404, sinking from y 60 to y 128, half hidden by the hills
- Top-right readout: ±0.0 EV → −2.0 EV
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
适用场景：When the shape and pose should carry the picture, against a sunset or a bright window.
