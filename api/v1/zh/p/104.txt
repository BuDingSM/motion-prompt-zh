请使用“蓝调时刻（Blue hour）”，它是“摄影风格、光线与镜头效果”中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #104

它是什么：日落后、日出前天空转为深蓝色的时段
示例展示：Once the sun has set, the sky turns two bands of deep blue, the windows and street lamp come on in amber and the person becomes a dark blue shape.
规格：
- Scene 1: a head-and-shoulders figure in front of a horizon at y 150 (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px, no face or hair). 
- Sun: radius 18 px at x 120, sinking from y 40 to y 190 while the effect reaches 70%, fading out between 40% and 80%
- Colour: sky #2C3B5E at the top and #4A6191 near the horizon, ground #283452, buildings #34446B, figure #1C2439
- Lights: one window in three and the lamp bulb turn #FFC75A; flat rings of 13 px and 8 px (#FFE3A3 at 35% and 60%) around the bulb
- Top-right time 12:00 → 18:35
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
适用场景：For city and night scenes that need both a blue sky and glowing lights.
