请使用“镜头耀光（Lens flare）”，它是“摄影风格、光线与镜头效果”中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #109

它是什么：强光进入镜头，产生光环与光斑
示例展示：As the sun comes out from behind a building, rays appear around it and a line of light dots, hexagons and a ring runs from the sun through the centre of the frame to the opposite side.
规格：
- Scene 2: a street corner; buildings, trees and street lamps (x 114, 292, 494) behind a road at y 132–162; the person stands front left (feet at x 176, y 200, 124 px tall). 
- Sun: radius 13 px, from behind a building (x 428, y 74) to open sky (x 474, y 30)
- Rays: 8, from 17 px out from the centre of the sun to 26 px or 44 px, alternating (9 px and 27 px long; amber, 1.6 px, 75%)
- Flare: on the line from the sun through the centre at t = 0.45, 0.8, 1.15, 1.45, 1.75 and 2.0 (t = 1 is the centre): a 7 px circle, a 13 px hexagon, a 5 px circle, a 22 px hexagon, a 9 px circle and a 30 px ring (3 px line)
- Colours amber #FFC75A and #FFE3A3 at 40–70% × the effect
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
适用场景：To add glare, summer sunshine or a cinematic light feel.
