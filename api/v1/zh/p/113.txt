请使用“High Key（高调）（High-key）”，它是“摄影风格、光线与镜头效果”中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #113

它是什么：只用明亮的色调，画面偏白、阴影很淡
示例展示：The landscape and the person turn to light, near-white colours with very faint shadows, and the hump of the histogram at the bottom right moves to the right end.
规格：
- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right. 
- Colour: sky #FAF9F7 → #FFFFFF, hills #EAEDF1 → #F5F6F8, figure #4D5A75 → #B9C0CE, shadow #33405A → #A6AEBF, sun #F5A524 → #FFD889
- Histogram (96×46 px, 20 bars): peak centre 0.56 → 0.85, width 0.2 → 0.09
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
适用场景：For clean, light, gentle product shots and portraits.
