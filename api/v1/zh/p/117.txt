请使用“Teal & Orange（Teal and orange）”，它是“摄影风格、光线与镜头效果”中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #117

它是什么：阴影偏青绿色，肤色与亮部偏橘色的色调
示例展示：In the same landscape the sky, hills, ground and the shadowed figure shift to teal while the sunlit edge of the figure and the sun shift to orange, a complementary contrast.
规格：
- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right. 
- Teal: sky #CFE6E3, hills #8CC1BE and #62A2A1, ground #3F8083, trees #2F6A6E, figure #1D4E53
- Orange: the light on the figure #FF9248, sun #FF8236, sun rings #FFC48C
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
适用场景：For a blockbuster, trailer-like look with strong colour contrast.
