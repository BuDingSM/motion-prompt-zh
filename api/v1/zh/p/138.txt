Motion Prompt #138

它是什么：The next scene opens through thin strips
示例展示：Gaps in a row of thin strips widen until the next scene is fully open.
规格：
- Day scene → night scene → day, 2.35 s each. Seven horizontal strips (208 ÷ 7 ≈ 29.7 px each).
- 0.4–1.65 s (1.25 s), quadratic ease-in-out: in every strip at once, the next scene is revealed from the strip's top edge downward, opening from 0 to the full strip height.
- The previous scene stays underneath. Neither picture moves.
适用场景：Use it for a clear switch between two scenes, such as day and night or before and after, or for a retro feel.
