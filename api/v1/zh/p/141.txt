Motion Prompt #141

它是什么：Turns into coarse blocks, then switches
示例展示：The picture turns into coarser and coarser blocks, switches at the coarsest point, then sharpens again.
规格：
- Field scene → city scene → field, 2.35 s each. At 0.2–1.9 s (1.7 s) the block size steps up and back down through 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 15, 20, 27 and 36 px (coarsest at the midpoint, 1.05 s).
- The first half shows the old scene and the second half the new one; the swap happens at the coarsest step.
- Method: shrink the frame to width ÷ block size with smoothing on, then scale it back up with smoothing off.
适用场景：Use it for game-like, retro or digital scene changes, or to blur something out as the scene switches.
